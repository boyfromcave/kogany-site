About

Kogany is built by the Ycash developers, under the direction of the Yellowback lead architect. It is the third node line for the Ycash vault upgrade, beside ycashd 4.5 and 6.20, and the Rust home of Ycash Yellowback (YED).

Design and sign-off

Sets the vault upgrade's design and decides its open questions. Signs off each node line as a release line once it passes the gates.

Relationship to Zakura

Kogany is a fork of Zakura, licensed as Zakura is. We keep its architecture, its upstream tests and its copyright notices. A build without the ycash feature still runs Zakura's own networks, which lets us show that the dormant vault code changes nothing there. Kogany is an independent project and is not endorsed by the Zakura team.

Ground rules

  • Consensus changes are reviewed: two people independent of the author review every consensus-critical line.
  • One change on every line: a shared vault or Yellowback rule change lands on all node lines together.
  • Nothing is trusted from an artifact: vault and Yellowback state are always computed, never imported.
  • Upstream automation stays off until each workflow has been reviewed for the fork.
