About Kogany

Kogany is built by the Ycash developers, under the direction of the Yellowback lead architect. It is part of the work to bring a decentralized digital dollar, Ycash Yellowback (YED), to Ycash through the vault network upgrade.

Relationship to Zakura

Kogany is a fork of Zakura, dual-licensed under MIT and Apache 2.0 as Zakura is. We keep Zakura's architecture, its upstream tests and its copyright notices. Zakura's networks still work in a build without the ycash feature, which lets us show that the dormant vault code changes nothing on Zcash. Kogany is an independent project and is not endorsed by the Zakura team.

Ground rules

  • Consensus changes are reviewed. Every consensus-critical line is reviewed by two people independent of its author.
  • One change on every line. A shared vault or Yellowback rule change lands on all node lines together.
  • Nothing is trusted from an artifact. Vault and Yellowback state are always computed, never imported.
  • Upstream automation stays off until each workflow has been reviewed for the fork.
