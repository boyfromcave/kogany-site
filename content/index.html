Kogany is a full node for Ycash, built on Zakura's Rust architecture. It adds Ycash's consensus rules, the vault network upgrade and the Yellowback (YED) rule module.

It is built by the Ycash developers as the third node line beside ycashd 4.5 and 6.20. Meet the team.

01

Quantum-resistant addresses

The vault upgrade adds addresses signed with FN-DSA-512 (Falcon) or SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s, two schemes from NIST's post-quantum standardization. They work for plain YEC, vault owners and YED, and the prefix names the scheme: sqf… or sqs… on mainnet, tqf… or tqs… on testnet. Live on the fast testnet since block 510,400.
