Yellowback (YED)

Ycash Yellowback (YED) is a decentralized dollar on Ycash, 1 YED = $1. You lock YEC in a vault to mint YED and burn the YED to get your YEC back. Its rules run as the one registered module, YED\0, on the vault network upgrade, and every full node checks them.

How it works

  • Mint: lock YEC in a vault and mint YED against it, within the collateral tiers (300, 400 or 500 %).
  • Redeem: burn YED to release the YEC, at any time. An early-release fee applies within the term (5, 2.5 or 1 %, by tier).
  • Claim: anyone can claim a position that falls below 125 % collateral.
  • Prices come from a bonded attestor set. Attestors sign prices and take no other on-chain action.
  • Owner recovery: if the signer set goes dormant, the owner can always get the YEC back. Recovering a YED position always burns the YED.
