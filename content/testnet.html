Public testnets

Testnet

Two public Ycash testnets run the vault upgrade: a fast one for quick experiments and a slow one that behaves more like mainnet. Testnet YEC is free and worthless.

Not deployed yet. The testnets are defined and their services built, but they have not been launched on public infrastructure. The figures below are the build-time configuration, not live readings.

The faucet

Each testnet has its own faucet. It pays a small fixed amount of testnet YEC (1 YEC by default) and never pays YED: you mint YED yourself, against a vault. To keep it fair, the faucet limits requests per visitor and per address, enforces a daily cap, and may ask your browser to solve a short proof-of-work challenge. It refuses to pay unless its node reports the testnet profile it was configured for.

The faucet button above appears once a network's faucet is live and announced in the status feed.

Joining with ycashd

Until Kogany syncs the Ycash chain (wave W1), join with ycashd 6.22.x from the vault upgrade line. The testnet profile is chosen at startup, and both nodes report it in getblockchaininfo as testnetprofile. Keep the RPC port bound to localhost.

Joining with Kogany

Kogany will support the same fast and slow profiles. It will not support the older legacy testnet. These instructions will be filled in, with the exact revision to build, once a Kogany build passes the testnet sync gate against ycashd.
